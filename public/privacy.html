Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Nature of Service

TempLine Inbox provides temporary, shared email aliases and SMS numbers for software testing, OTP verification, and spam prevention. Messages received on TempLine are temporary, stored only in volatile memory, and automatically cleared when the server restarts or becomes inactive.

2. Public & Non-Confidential Warning

Addresses listed on TempLine are shared public utilities. Do not use TempLine for banking, government IDs, personal accounts, password resets for important services, or confidential communications.

3. Cookies, Local Storage & Advertising Partners

We use browser localStorage solely to remember any custom email aliases you save on your device. We also display third-party advertisements and affiliate links to keep this service free. Third-party ad networks may use cookies or anonymous identifiers to serve relevant ads and measure impressions.
